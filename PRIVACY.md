# Privacy — Battle of Blood: Arena

Battle of Blood: Arena does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- No accounts, no sign-in, no email, no names.
- No cookies, analytics, fingerprinting or third-party trackers in the game code.
- Game progress (coins, unlocked skins, settings) is saved only in your own browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it. You can also use **Settings → Reset progress**.
- The game loads the open-source three.js engine from the jsDelivr CDN.
- When played on a game portal (e.g. CrazyGames or Poki), that portal's own SDK may show ads and is covered by the portal's privacy policy.
