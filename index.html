    How to play

    Survive wave after wave. Every wave you clear pays coins, refills ammo and brings downed teammates back.

    Mobile

    Left side moves, right side looks. FIRE, AIM, JUMP, R, SWAP, MED, WALL, MINE and MAP are on screen; a USE button appears next to ziplines, the jeep and levers.

    Tips

    • Main villains: each island has one — VIRAAT the Titan King, BHAIRAV the Abyss Beast and ZORVAK the Xeno Overlord. A siren sounds and the world turns red when one arrives (every 3rd wave). Rockets, bombs, the flamethrower and grapple slashes on the nape are your best tools.
    • Airdrops: a cargo plane drops a red supply crate every minute or so — it refills everything and can give you new guns and a loaner jetpack.
    • Ammo: yellow AMMO crates are spread over every map and more drop each wave. Villains drop ammo often.
    • Giants: slow but deadly. Their slam is shown by an orange ring on the ground — step out of it. Headshots do extra damage. They also throw rocks from range.
    • Beasts: wolves hunt in packs, tigers pounce from about 10 m, bears soak up bullets. Keep moving.
    • KAAL: three times during the fight he kneels, stunned and glowing — every hit then does double damage. His stomp is shown by a purple ring. Supplies drop when he calls drones.
    • Ziplines: ride from hilltops into the town and shoot while you fly. Space or F lets go.
    • Map: the radar shows villains (red), beasts (orange), giants (yellow G), KAAL (purple K), friends (blue arrows) and ammo (yellow).

    Privacy policy

    Battle of Blood: Arena · last updated 16 November 2026

    Battle of Blood: Arena is an original browser game made by Sauravh Yadav. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

    The short version

    • No account or sign-up is needed to play.
    • The game does not collect, sell or share your personal information.
    • The game contains no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own.

    What is saved on your device

    Your progress (coins, rank, unlocked skins and weapons, missions, settings and the squad nickname you type) is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device, so it is there next time you play. When you play on CrazyGames, the same progress may also be saved by CrazyGames' cloud save. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

    Online co-op

    When you join a squad on the game's claude.ai link, the nickname you choose, your operator skin and your live in-game state (position, aim direction, health and shots) are shared in real time with the other players using the same squad code. This is needed to show you to your friends. It is not stored by the game and disappears when you leave the match. Please don't use your real full name or other personal details as a nickname. The claude.ai link is hosted by Anthropic, and your claude.ai account is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

    Game portals and ads

    If you play on a game portal such as CrazyGames or Poki, that portal may show ads (for example an optional “watch ad” revive) and may process data under its own privacy policy. Please read the portal's policy for details.

    Children

    The game contains fantasy combat and is intended for players aged 13 and up. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

    Changes and contact

    If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions? Contact the developer through GitHub: github.com/sauravh-ledger.
